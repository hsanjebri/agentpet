AgentPet

A desktop pet that babysits your AI coding agent. Noodle sleeps while Claude Code is idle, hops when it needs your approval (approve right from his bubble), and farts a rainbow when the task is done. 🌈

What Noodle does

✅ Approve from the cat

When Claude asks for permission, Noodle hops toward you and shows what it wants to run. Approve or deny right there.

🐱 His tail tells you

Sleeping, working (coffee mug), needs you (red antenna), done (rainbow), sweating, sad, hissing. One glance and you know.

📱 Phone ping

Walked away? If Claude waits for you a few minutes, your phone buzzes. Free, no account.

📊 Session usage bar

Your 5-hour Claude limit under his paws, green → yellow → red. He sweats at 80%.

😾 Risk alerts

He hisses at .env, rm -rf, git push --force, deleted tests, and says why.

🎵 Vibes to your music

Headphones on, head bobbing to Spotify or YouTube, with pixel play/pause buttons. (Windows)

🌈 Recap & Wrapped

A daily card and a weekly “Wrapped”: tasks, approvals, your favorite file, the scariest moment. Share it.

🎩 He grows up

Kitten → cat → chonky senior with glasses. Unlock a top hat, sunglasses and a crown. Pet him, feed him, name him.

🔒 Private by design

Runs on your computer only. No account, no telemetry. Your Claude settings are backed up before any change.

🛡️ “Windows protected your PC”? That’s expected (for now)

AgentPet isn’t code-signed yet. Signing certificates cost money every year, and we’ll add one as the project grows. Until then Windows and macOS warn about any unsigned app. The code is open, so you can read it or build it yourself.

  • Windows: click More info, then Run anyway.
  • macOS: right-click AgentPet › Open, or System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway.
  • Linux: make the AppImage executable (chmod +x AgentPet.AppImage) and run it.
