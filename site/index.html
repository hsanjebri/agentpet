Questions

Is Noodle really free?

Yes. Free and open source (MIT), on Windows, Mac and Linux. No ads, no account.

Do I need to be a developer?

No. At setup pick "Just for fun & focus": music, Pomodoro, habits and a cat that grows with you. The Claude Code features only appear if you pick "developer".

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is that safe?

That's expected for now: Noodle isn't code-signed yet (certificates cost money every year). Click More info, then Run anyway. On Mac: right-click › Open. The source code is public if you want to check it.

What does he send over the internet?

Nothing, unless you turn a feature on. Weather sends your city's coordinates, Friends shares your pet's name, skin and level with friends you accept, and phone ping sends a project name to your own ntfy topic. That's the full list.

How do friends work?

Turn Friends on in Settings and you get a code like NOODLE-48K2. Give it to a friend, they add it, you accept, and you see each other's cat with a green dot when they're online. No email, no password: an anonymous account is made for you. Only the pet's name, skin, level and online status are shared.

Does he slow my computer down?

No. He's a tiny window that sleeps most of the time. You can also make him smaller, or hide him automatically during fullscreen apps and meetings.
